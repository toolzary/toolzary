1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

They help websites remember preferences, understand usage, and improve functionality.

2. How We Use Cookies

Toolzary may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website performance and reliability.
  • Remember user preferences such as display settings.
  • Understand how visitors interact with our website.
  • Help improve existing tools and create better features.

3. Types Of Cookies

The cookies used on Toolzary may include:

Essential Cookies

These cookies help basic website functions work properly.

Preference Cookies

These cookies remember choices such as theme preferences or settings.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies may help us understand website usage and improve user experience.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services used on Toolzary may place their own cookies according to their privacy policies.

Examples may include analytics, advertising, or embedded external services.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality or user experience.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
