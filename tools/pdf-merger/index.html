About PDF Merger

Toolzary's PDF Merger is a free online tool that lets you combine multiple PDF files into a single PDF document. Add your PDF files, arrange them in the order you want, and merge them with just one click.

The merger processes your PDF files directly in your browser. Your selected documents do not need to be uploaded to a server, making it convenient for combining documents while keeping your files on your device.

Whether you need to combine reports, assignments, invoices, presentations, or other PDF documents, Toolzary's PDF Merger provides a simple way to create one organized PDF file without registration or installation.

How To Use

Select PDF Files

Choose two or more PDF files from your device or drag them into the upload area.

Arrange Your PDFs

Drag the PDF files to arrange them in the order you want them to appear.

Merge & Download

Click Merge PDFs and download your combined PDF file when processing is complete.
