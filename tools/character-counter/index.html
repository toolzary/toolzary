About Character Counter

This tool provides detailed character analysis of your text, including total characters, letters, numbers, spaces, emojis, special characters, lines, words, sentences, and paragraphs. It uses Unicode aware regular expressions to correctly handle all scripts, emojis, and accented characters. All processing is done locally in your browser, no data is ever sent to a server.

Emoji Support: Emojis are counted as single characters in total character count. Special Characters: All non-letter, non-number, non-space characters are tracked separately. Privacy: Your text never leaves your device.
