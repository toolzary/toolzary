About Word Counter

This advanced word counter provides comprehensive text analysis including total words, unique words, character counts, sentence and paragraph detection, average word length, reading time, and word frequency distribution. Perfect for writers, students, bloggers, and content creators. All processing is done locally in your browser, your text never leaves your device.

Reading Time: Calculated at an average reading speed of 200 words per minute. Unique Words: Counts distinct words (case-insensitive). Word Frequency: Shows how many times each word appears.

Everything you need to know about word counting

The word counter detects words by analyzing whitespace and punctuation while correctly handling emojis, symbols, and different Unicode scripts.

Total Words counts every word occurrence. Unique Words counts distinct words (case-insensitive). For example, "The cat and the dog" has 5 total words but 4 unique words because "the" appears twice.

Reading time is estimated using an average reading speed of 200 words per minute. For example, 500 words takes about 2.5 minutes to read.
