About Color Palette Generator

Generate beautiful color palettes based on a base color. Choose from five different palette types: Monochromatic, Analogous, Complementary, Triadic, and Tetradic. Perfect for designers, developers, and creatives.

Monochromatic: Variations of a single hue
Analogous: Colors adjacent on the color wheel
Complementary: Opposite colors on the color wheel
Triadic: Three evenly spaced colors
Tetradic: Four colors forming a rectangle

Everything you need to know about color palettes

A color palette is a collection of colors that work well together. It's used in design to create visual harmony and consistency.

Monochromatic: Same hue, different shades.
Analogous: Adjacent colors on the color wheel.
Complementary: Opposite colors.
Triadic: Three evenly spaced colors.
Tetradic: Four colors forming a rectangle.
