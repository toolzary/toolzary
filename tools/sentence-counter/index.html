About Sentence Counter

This advanced sentence counter provides comprehensive text analysis including total sentences, words per sentence, character counts, sentence length analysis, and more. Perfect for writers, students, editors, and content creators. All processing is done locally in your browser, your text never leaves your device.

Sentence Detection: Identifies sentences using ., !, and ? while protecting abbreviations. Unique Words: Counts distinct words (case-insensitive). Reading Time: Calculated at an average reading speed of 200 words per minute.
