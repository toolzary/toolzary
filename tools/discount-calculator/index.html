Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about discount calculations

To calculate a discount, multiply the original price by the discount percentage (as a decimal). For example, 20% off $100 = $100 × 0.20 = $20 savings. Final price = $100 - $20 = $80.

"Percentage Off" applies a percentage discount (e.g., 20% off). "Amount Off" applies a fixed dollar discount (e.g., $20 off). Both give you the final price and savings.

To find the discount percentage, divide the savings by the original price and multiply by 100. For example, $20 savings on $100 = (20 ÷ 100) × 100 = 20% discount.
