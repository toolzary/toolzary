About BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight. This calculator supports multiple units: weight (kg, g, lbs, stones, oz) and height (cm, m, inches, feet, mm). BMI Categories: Underweight (<18.5), Normal (18.5-24.9), Overweight (25-29.9), Obese (≥30).

Everything you need to know about BMI calculation

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measurement that uses your height and weight to estimate body fat. It's a simple, widely used screening tool for weight categories.

Formula: weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²). This calculator automatically converts your selected units to metric for accurate calculation.

BMI is a general screening tool but may not be accurate for athletes (more muscle mass), pregnant women, or elderly people. Consult a doctor for professional assessment.
