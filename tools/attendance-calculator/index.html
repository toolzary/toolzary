About Attendance Calculator

Toolzary's Attendance Calculator helps students calculate their attendance percentage instantly. Enter your total classes and attended classes to check your current attendance status.

The calculator also shows how many additional classes you need to attend to reach your target percentage and how many classes you can still miss while maintaining your goal.

Whether you are a school student, college student, or teacher, this tool provides fast and accurate attendance calculations directly in your browser without storing your data.

How To Use

Enter Class Details

Enter the total number of classes held and the number of classes you have attended.

Set Target Percentage

Choose your required attendance percentage. The default target is 75%.

Get Attendance Report

Calculate your attendance percentage instantly using your class records.
