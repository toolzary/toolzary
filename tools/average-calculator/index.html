About Average Calculator

Toolzary's Average Calculator helps you quickly calculate the arithmetic mean of any set of numbers. Simply enter your values one by one and get instant results.

Along with the average, the calculator also displays the total sum, number of values, minimum value, maximum value, and median to give you a complete statistical summary.

Whether you're a student, teacher, researcher, accountant, or anyone working with numbers, this free online calculator performs all calculations instantly without uploading or storing your data.
