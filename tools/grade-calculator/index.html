Everything you need to know about grade calculation

GPA is calculated by multiplying each course grade points by its credit hours, summing them, and dividing by total credit hours. Different scales (4.0, 5.0, 100-point) are supported.

Weighted grade considers the importance of each assignment. For example, a final exam worth 30% of your grade has more impact than a quiz worth 10%.

Enter your current grade, desired grade, and final exam weight. The calculator will tell you the exact score you need on your final to achieve your goal.
