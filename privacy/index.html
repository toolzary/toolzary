1. Overview

Toolzary respects your privacy and is committed to providing a safe and transparent experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website or use our online tools.

2. Information We Collect

Toolzary is designed to work with minimal data collection.

Depending on how you use the website, information that may be collected includes:

  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device information, or general usage statistics.
  • Information provided voluntarily when contacting us.
  • Anonymous usage information used to improve website performance.

3. Tool Data Processing

Many Toolzary tools process information directly inside your browser.

We do not intentionally store user-entered data from tools unless a specific feature clearly states otherwise.

Users should avoid entering confidential, sensitive, or private information into online tools unless they understand the risks.

4. Cookies And Analytics

Toolzary may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand usage patterns, and provide a better experience.

You can manage cookie settings through your browser preferences.

5. Third-Party Services

Toolzary may use third-party services such as analytics, advertising, hosting, or external resources.

These third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain a secure website environment.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security, and users should use online services responsibly.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be available on this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Toolzary handles information, please contact us through our Contact page.
